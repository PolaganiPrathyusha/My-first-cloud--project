# Day 2: Computer Vision on Azure

**Date:** 11 Aug 2026  
**Topic:** Azure AI Vision

## What I learned:
- **Computer Vision** = AI that can "see" and understand images
- **Azure AI Vision services:**
    - **Image Analysis** - describe images, detect objects, captions
    - **Face API** - detect faces, age, emotions
    - **OCR / Read** - extract text from images and documents  
    - **Custom Vision** - train your own image classifier
- **Real use cases:** Accessibility tools, security, retail, healthcare

## Key Azure service:
`Azure AI Vision` - Part of Azure AI services

## My notes:
AI can describe what's in a photo and read text from images. This is used in apps for visually impaired people.
